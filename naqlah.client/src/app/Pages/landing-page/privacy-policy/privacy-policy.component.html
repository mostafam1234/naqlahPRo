Privacy Policy – Naqlat Customer App

Effective date: June 8, 2026

App package: sa.nuqlat.customer

This Privacy Policy explains how Naqlat ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your personal data when you use the Naqlat Customer mobile application and related services (the "App" or "Service"). The App is a Saudi logistics and delivery platform that allows customers to create delivery orders, track shipments, manage payments, and communicate with our team.

By registering for or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who We Are

Naqlat operates a logistics and delivery service in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. For privacy-related inquiries, you may contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@naqlat.sa
  • Support: support@naqlat.sa
  • Website: https://naqlat.sa

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account and identity information: full name, phone number, email address (if provided), national ID or identity number, commercial registration details (for business accounts), establishment name, and representative information.
  • Verification documents: photos or images of identity documents, commercial registration, or other documents you upload during registration or account verification.
  • Location data: precise and approximate location, including pickup and delivery addresses, live order tracking, route information, and background location when required to provide delivery services and order updates.
  • Order and transaction data: order details, shipment information, delivery history, invoices, wallet balance, payment method selections, and transaction records.
  • Communication data: messages sent through in-app chat or support features, complaints, suggestions, and customer service interactions.
  • Device and technical data: device model, operating system, unique device identifiers, push notification tokens, IP address, app version, language settings, and crash/error logs.
  • Usage data: how you interact with the App, including screens viewed, features used, and session activity.
  • Permissions-based data: information accessed through device permissions such as camera, photo library, phone state, and notifications, only when you grant such permissions.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data to:

  • Create and manage your customer account.
  • Process, assign, deliver, and track logistics and delivery orders.
  • Verify your identity and comply with legal and regulatory requirements.
  • Calculate pricing, process payments, and manage your wallet.
  • Send order updates, delivery notifications, and service-related alerts.
  • Provide customer support and respond to complaints or inquiries.
  • Improve App performance, security, reliability, and user experience.
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, misuse, or unauthorized activity.
  • Comply with applicable laws, court orders, and government requests.
  • Send important service announcements or policy updates.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data based on one or more of the following grounds, as applicable under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and other applicable regulations:

  • Your consent, where required.
  • Performance of a contract or provision of the Service you request.
  • Compliance with legal obligations.
  • Protection of your vital interests or those of another person.
  • Our legitimate interests, such as improving service quality and ensuring platform security, where such interests are not overridden by your rights.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only as necessary with:

  • Delivery partners and drivers to fulfill your orders (for example, name, phone number, pickup/delivery locations, and order details).
  • Payment and financial service providers to process transactions securely.
  • Cloud hosting and IT service providers that support our backend systems and data storage.
  • Analytics, crash reporting, and messaging providers, including services such as Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config, Performance Monitoring, Firestore, and Storage) and Google Maps/Places, to operate and improve the App.
  • Legal and regulatory authorities when required by applicable law or to protect our rights, users, or the public.
  • Business transfers in connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate safeguards.

All third-party service providers are required to handle your data securely and only for the purposes we authorize.

6. Location Data

The App uses location services to help you select addresses, create delivery requests, and track active orders. With your permission, location data may be collected while the App is in use and, where necessary for delivery operations, in the background. You can manage location permissions through your device settings, but disabling location access may limit core App functionality.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide the Service, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When data is no longer required, we will delete or anonymize it in accordance with our retention policies and applicable law.

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Saudi PDPL, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your data, where legally permitted.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities.
  • Request a copy of your data in a usable format, where applicable.

To exercise your rights, contact us at privacy@naqlat.sa. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

11. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed or stored on servers located inside or outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia through our service providers. Where data is transferred internationally, we take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place as required by applicable law.

12. Third-Party Links and Services

The App may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you through the App, by email, or by other appropriate means. The updated version will be effective once published, and your continued use of the App after the update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
